# NZ agricultural work-visa landscape (pastoral / dairy / sheep-&-beef)

Current as of 2026. Figures from immigration.govt.nz, MBIE, DairyNZ. Flags on fast-moving items inline.

## The primary pathway: AEWV (Accredited Employer Work Visa)

A **3-step, employer-led** process — it replaced the Essential Skills visa and is how almost all
pastoral/dairy migrant hiring runs.

| Step | Who | What | Cost (verify live) | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) Employer accreditation** | Employer | Prove a genuine, financially sustainable, compliant business. Types: Standard (≤5 migrants), High-Volume (6+), Triangular/labour-hire. First grant ~12 mo, then renewable 24 mo. | Std ~$775 · HV ~$1,280 · Triangular ~$4,060 | ~10 working days |
| **(b) Job check** | Employer | Per role. Genuine role, lawful terms, **market-rate pay**, and — for most roles — **advertised ≥3 weeks + labour-market test** (engage MSD/Work & Income). | ~$735 per check | days–2 wks |
| **(c) Migrant visa** | Worker | Tied to the checked job; evidence of **2 years' work experience** + skills, health, character, English where applicable. | ~$1,540 (app + levy) + IVL | ~4–6 wks |

**Advertising/LMT exemptions:** Green List roles, roles paid **≥2× median (~$70/hr)**, and some
sector arrangements skip advertising.

### AEWV changes that matter (2024–2026)
- **10 Mar 2025** — median-wage requirement **removed**; pay the **NZ market rate** (min-wage floor only). Work-experience cut 3→**2 years**. Lower-skilled (ANZSCO 4–5) max continuous stay 2→**3 years**.
- **8 Dec 2025** — new **Peak Seasonal Visa** covers dairy **calf rearers / relief milkers** (7-month peak-season visa; 4-month stand-down) — the first seasonal route for dairy.
- **9 Mar 2026** — median wage → **$35.00/hr** (recalculates the 1.5× 5-yr-stay and 2× skip-ads triggers + residence checks).
- **24 Aug 2026** — SMC overhaul: new Skilled Work Experience + Trades/Technician residence pathways; wage-rate grace period.

## Median wage / wage rules
- **Median wage $35.00/hr** (from 9 Mar 2026). Minimum wage floor $23.50/hr.
- **No AEWV median-wage floor** since Mar 2025 (market rate instead); **no dairy-specific exemption** (that scheme ended and never included dairy — a myth to correct).
- Median still governs: **5-year stay** on lower-skilled roles (≥1.5× ≈ $52.50), **skip-advertising** (≥2× ≈ $70), and residence pay checks.

## RSE — scope confirmation
**Recognised Seasonal Employer is horticulture/viticulture ONLY, Pacific workers.** It does **NOT**
cover pastoral, dairy, or sheep-&-beef. Never apply RSE to a farm employer. (Season cap 20,750 for 2024/25.)

## Working Holiday Visas
Young people (~18–30/35) from ~45 countries; usually 12 mo (UK 36, Canada 23). Can take **any job**
incl. farm work — no employer accreditation needed. The realistic channel for **casual/relief/seasonal**
dairy & sheep-&-beef labour. Note the 3-month seasonal-work **extension trigger is hort/viti, not dairy.**

## Residence pathways
- **Green List, Tier 2 (Work-to-Residence):** **Dairy Cattle Farmer — ANZSCO 121313** — covers
  **Dairy Farm Manager, Assistant Dairy Farm Manager, Dairy Herd Manager** (herd manager and above).
  Work the role on an AEWV for the qualifying period → apply for residence. *(Exact dairy time-in-role
  — standard Tier-2 is 24 months, DairyNZ frames dairy as ~3 yrs — and pay threshold: verify on the live INZ Green List page.)*
- **Farm assistants are NOT on the Green List** — no direct residence route (progress to herd manager+, or SMC).
- **Skilled Migrant Category** — 6-point system (quals, income vs median, registration, NZ experience). Higher-skilled/paid dairy managers can qualify; general assistants usually can't.

## Role → visa map (dairy)

| Role | Typical visa | Max stay | Residence pathway |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calf rearer / relief milker | Peak Seasonal Visa (from Dec 2025) | 7 mo + 4-mo stand-down | None directly |
| Farm assistant | AEWV | 3 years | Not Green List; SMC unlikely |
| Herd manager / assistant manager | AEWV (ANZSCO 121313) | up to 5 years | **Green List Tier 2** |
| Dairy farm manager | AEWV (ANZSCO 121313) | up to 5 years | **Green List Tier 2** |

Sheep-&-beef equivalents are AEWV but **not confirmed Green-listed** — treat as AEWV-only until checked per role.
