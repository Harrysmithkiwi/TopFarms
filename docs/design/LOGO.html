The gable mark.

Not the generic leaf. Two nested peaks in a rounded tile: a paddock gable (the roofline of a woolshed) sitting over a contour ridge. It carries two of the brand motifs at once, survives to 16px, and never needs a photograph. One green, flat, no gradient.

Primary, mono, reversed

Three sanctioned forms. The colour mark on light, a single-ink mono for stamps and faxable contexts, and the reversed mark on the brand dark #0F3D22 for the footer bookend.

Clear space & misuse

Keep clear space equal to the tile's corner radius on all sides. Minimum mark size 16px; minimum lockup height 20px.

TopFarms
✕ no second colour
The mark is one green plus ink and white. Never a gradient, never a second hue.
✕ no drop shadow
Flat only. Depth comes from the tile fill, not a shadow.
✕ don't stretch or rotate
The gable stays upright and square. It is a building, not a logo doing tricks.
