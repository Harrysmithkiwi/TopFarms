Recommendation, from the renders
Ship Fraunces 500 as display, Inter for everything else — but only if the discipline holds. The renders change one thing versus arguing it on paper: Inter-800 is closer than expected. It is not the fallback; it is a legitimate answer that trades all warmth for zero risk and zero added bytes. So the real decision is a brand call, not a type call — does TopFarms want warm-and-editorial (Fraunces, higher ceiling, needs restraint to not become the cliché) or plain-and-bombproof (Inter-800, lower ceiling, cannot embarrass us)? My pick is Fraunces because the whole redesign thesis is "make it look decided," and a warm serif set with restraint is the most decided-looking move on the board. If the founder wants the safest possible launch, Inter-800 is the honest second and I will not argue.
