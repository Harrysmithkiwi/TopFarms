- **Topic:** farm-worker retention — why good people leave and the 3 things that keep them.
- **Project / brand:** TopFarms (farm employment + upskilling; "sustainable careers").
- **Brand source consumed:** `docs/_canonical/Brand_and_Design.md` (canonical greens + Inter + voice — referenced, not duplicated).
- **Slides:** 8 · **Format:** 1080×1350 portrait.
- **Files:** `copy.md` (slide copy), `prompts.md` (one model-neutral image prompt per slide).

## How to ship
1. Paste each prompt in `prompts.md` into your chosen image model (text-heavy → prefer a
   strong text renderer; as of 2026-06, Gemini "Nano Banana Pro" — re-check before relying).
2. Generate all 8 slide images at 1080×1350.
3. Combine the 8 images into one PDF, upload to LinkedIn as a **document post**.
