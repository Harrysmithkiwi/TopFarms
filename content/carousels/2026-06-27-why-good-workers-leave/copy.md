# Carousel copy — "Why your best workers leave after one season"

**Brand source:** `~/dev/topfarms/docs/_canonical/Brand_and_Design.md` (canonical — consumed, not duplicated).
**Voice:** confident, grounded, Kiwi trade tool; warm; verb-first CTAs. Audience: farm employers/operators.
**Slides:** 8 · **Format:** 1080×1350.

## Slide 1 — Hook
**Headline:** You'll lose 1 in 3 of your best workers this season.
**Body:** And it's probably not the pay. →

## Slide 2 — The real cost
**Headline:** A good worker walking out costs more than you think.
**Body:** Recruiting, training, lost output, and all the know-how that leaves with them. Replacing beats keeping — every time.

## Slide 3 — The myth
**Headline:** "They left for two bucks more an hour."
**Body:** Usually not. Good people leave when there's nowhere to go — not for a slightly bigger pay packet.

## Slide 4 — Keep them #1
**Headline:** Show them a path.
**Body:** People stay where they can level up. A clear way to learn and step up beats a one-off raise.

## Slide 5 — Keep them #2
**Headline:** See the person.
**Body:** Good management and a genuine "good on ya" outlast any bonus. Being noticed is why people stay.

## Slide 6 — Keep them #3
**Headline:** Make it steady and safe.
**Body:** Predictable hours and a safe site tell a worker they've got a future here — not just a season.

## Slide 7 — The payoff
**Headline:** Build careers, not just seasons.
**Body:** Retention is cheaper than recruitment, and it compounds. Keep your best people and the whole operation lifts.

## Slide 8 — CTA
**Headline:** Start keeping your best people.
**Body:** Follow TopFarms for the farm-employer playbook. Comment "KEEP" for the retention checklist.
